# Lab: place an order

Placing an order touches three services: **Orders**, **Inventory** and **Payments** (which calls an external card
provider). Keep them consistent when any step can fail or time out.

> **Interactive version:** [open the Place an order lab](#/labs/place-an-order) for the decisions as checked steps.

## Step 1: Saga steps and compensations

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

1. Orders: create order `pending` (compensate: `cancelled`).
2. Inventory: reserve items with a 15-minute expiry (compensate: release).
3. Payments: authorise the card with idempotency key = order id (compensate: void).
4. Orders: `confirmed`. Then capture the payment and ship, asynchronously.
</details>

## Step 2: Orchestration

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

An orchestrator (a workflow engine such as Temporal) runs the steps, retries with backoff, applies timeouts, and runs
compensations in reverse on failure. Its state survives crashes.
</details>

## Step 3: Reliable events

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Each service writes its state change and its event through an **outbox**; consumers keep an **inbox** of processed
event ids.
</details>

## Step 4: The payment timeout

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

A timeout means "unknown". Query the provider by idempotency key (or retry with the same key) before deciding. Never
compensate on a timeout alone; keep the order `pending` until the outcome is known.
</details>
