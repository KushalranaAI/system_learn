# Lab: split the shop

An online shop's monolith is slowing down 40 engineers in 6 teams. Deploys queue up for days, and a bug in
recommendations took checkout down twice last month. Plan the first services to extract.

> **Interactive version:** [open the Split the shop lab](#/labs/split-the-shop) to make the key decisions as checked
> steps.

## Step 1: Pick boundaries

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Business capabilities: Catalogue, Inventory, Ordering (cart + orders), Payments, Fulfilment, Accounts, plus
Recommendations. Each owns its tables.
</details>

## Step 2: Which to extract first?

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

**Recommendations**: it has caused outages, is read-only with respect to the core, and has a small interface ("give me
products for user X"). Calls to it get a short timeout and a fallback (show bestsellers). Next: **Payments**, which has
clear ownership, strict compliance needs, and a small stable interface.
</details>

## Step 3: Data

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Recommendations consumes events (`ProductViewed`, `OrderPlaced`) and keeps its own store. No reading the monolith's
tables. During migration, the monolith publishes events from an outbox (Module 16).
</details>

## Step 4: Observability before the split

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Request ids at the gateway, OpenTelemetry tracing across the monolith and the new services, and RED dashboards per
service, all in place *before* traffic moves.
</details>
