# Lab: live analytics

A news site wants a live editors' dashboard: **views per article per minute**, the **top 10 articles right now**, and
**unique readers today**, from 80,000 page-view events per second, with results within 10 seconds.

> **Interactive version:** [open the Live analytics lab](#/labs/live-analytics) for the decisions as checked steps.

## Step 1: Ingest

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Browsers send view beacons to a collector API (202 Accepted, batched) → Kafka topic `pageviews`, keyed by
`article_id`, 64 partitions, 3-day retention.
</details>

## Step 2: Views per article per minute

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

A Flink job: tumbling 1-minute **event-time** windows keyed by article; watermark ~5 s; allowed lateness 2 min (with
corrections). Output upserts `(article, minute) → views` into a serving store (Redis or Postgres).
</details>

## Step 3: Top 10 now

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

A sliding 5-minute window updated every 10 s. Per-partition top-K, then a global merge (a second, single-partition
stage). Publish to a compacted topic the dashboard reads via SSE (Module 6).
</details>

## Step 4: Unique readers today

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

A HyperLogLog per article per day (about 12 KB each, ~1% error; lesson 19.4), merged across partitions. Exact uniques,
if ever needed, come from the nightly batch job.
</details>

## Step 5: Correctness

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Checkpointing every 30 s with exactly-once sinks; bot filtering before counting; a nightly batch recompute of
yesterday that overwrites the stream's numbers (reconciliation).
</details>
