# Lab: make a storefront feel instant

A shop has two weeks before a big sale. Search sends a request per keystroke, product pages come straight from two API
servers, and a slow recommendations service drags product pages down.

> **Interactive version:** [open the storefront lab](#/labs/api-instant-storefront) for the debounce simulation, a
> cache-header quiz, a CDN on the canvas and a circuit-breaker decision.

## Step 1: Search-as-you-type

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Debounce at ~150 ms, start at 2 characters, abort or ignore stale replies, and cache prefix results on the client.
Server side, `GET /suggest` reads a precomputed prefix index and is cached at the CDN for popular prefixes.
</details>

## Step 2: Cache headers

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Products: `public, max-age=60, s-maxage=300, stale-while-revalidate=300` + ETag, purge by tag on change. Cart:
`private, no-cache`. Checkout: `no-store`. Assets: `immutable` with hashed names.
</details>

## Step 3: Sale day

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Put a CDN in front of the load balancer. With an 80% hit ratio, two servers carry the remaining 8,000 req/s with
headroom. Pre-warm the most popular product pages before the sale starts.
</details>

## Step 4: The slow dependency

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

A 200-300 ms timeout on the recommendations call, a circuit breaker with a bestsellers fallback, or load
recommendations after the page renders. No retries on a dependency that's overloaded.
</details>
