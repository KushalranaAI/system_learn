# Lab: video upload processing

Creators upload videos. Each must be transcoded into 4 resolutions, get a thumbnail, be scanned, and the creator must
be notified when it's live.

> **Interactive version:** [open the Process uploads lab](#/labs/upload-pipeline) to absorb a 4x upload burst with a
> queue and workers on the simulator.

## Requirements

- Upload accepted in < 1 s (after the bytes arrive).
- The video goes live within 10 minutes normally, and within 1 hour during peaks.
- No job is lost; nothing is published twice; one corrupt file must not block others.

## Step 1: Split the pipeline

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

```
POST /videos → store original in object storage → insert video(status=uploaded) → enqueue "process"
"process" → enqueue transcode×4 (one per resolution) + thumbnail + scan
when all done → status=live → enqueue "notify"
```
Independent jobs retry independently; four transcodes run in parallel.
</details>

## Step 2: Make every job idempotent

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

- Transcodes write to deterministic paths (`videos/42/720p.mp4`), so a rerun overwrites the same file.
- Status changes are conditional (`UPDATE … SET status='live' WHERE id=42 AND status='processing'`).
- Notifications are claimed with a unique `(video_id, type)` row before sending.
</details>

## Step 3: Failures

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Exponential backoff with jitter; at most 6 attempts; then a dead-letter queue with an alert. Corrupt files fail fast
with a permanent error: mark the video `failed` and tell the creator. Long transcodes extend their lease and
checkpoint per segment.
</details>

## Step 4: Peaks

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Separate queues: `transcode` (heavy, autoscaled on backlog with a max), `notify` (light, high priority). Alert when the
oldest transcode job is older than 30 minutes. If the backlog keeps growing, temporarily skip the 4K rendition (degrade)
rather than delaying every video.
</details>
