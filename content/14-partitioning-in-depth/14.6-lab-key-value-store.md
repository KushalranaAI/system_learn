# Lab: a key-value store

Design a Dynamo-style key-value store for a shopping-cart service: **60,000 reads/s, 30,000 writes/s**, always
writable even when nodes fail, carts never lost.

> **Interactive version:** [open the Key-value store lab](#/labs/key-value-store) to scale writes with shards on the
> simulator, then make the partitioning and replication decisions.

## Step 1: Partitioning

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Consistent hashing with ~256 virtual nodes per physical node. Partition key = `cart_id`. Adding a node moves ~1/N of
the keys, spread evenly.
</details>

## Step 2: Replication and quorums

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

N = 3 replicas on distinct nodes in different zones (walking the ring clockwise). W = 2, R = 2 (W + R > N). Hinted
handoff keeps writes working when a replica is down; read repair and Merkle-tree anti-entropy heal stale replicas.
</details>

## Step 3: Conflicts

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Carts can be edited from two devices concurrently. Track versions with version vectors and merge concurrent versions
as an **OR-Set** of items (adds win; removes only remove what they saw), so items aren't silently lost (lesson 13.7).
</details>

## Step 4: Membership and failure detection

<details>
<summary>Reference</summary>

Gossip membership and the ring layout every second (lesson 26.6). A phi-accrual failure detector marks nodes suspect
or down. Permanent membership changes (adding or removing nodes) are explicit operator actions, so a flapping node
doesn't trigger a rebalance.
</details>
