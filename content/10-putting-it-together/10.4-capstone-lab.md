# Capstone lab

Two capstones: one guided on the simulator, one open-ended on paper.

## Part A: guided (simulator)

**[Open the Link shortener lab](#/labs/link-shortener)**: 10 steps that use everything from Part 1. API design and the
301-vs-302 tradeoff; launching at 300 req/s; scaling up on a budget; scaling out past one machine; caching at 60k
reads/s; stopping a scraper; surviving a server crash and a database crash.

## Part B: open-ended (paper, then explain)

> **Design a concert ticket-booking system.** Big concerts go on sale at a set time; up to 1M fans arrive in the first
> minute for 50,000 seats. Nobody may get a seat that someone else also got, and nobody may be charged without a
> ticket.

Use the seven-step framework (lesson 10.1). Time-box it to 45 minutes, then record a 10-minute explanation
(lesson 10.2).

<details>
<summary>Reference outline (open only after you've tried)</summary>

**Requirements:** browse events; hold a seat for 10 minutes while paying; pay; get a ticket. Non-functional: no double
booking (correctness above all), survive the on-sale spike, p99 < 1 s for the hold step, fairness.

**Estimates:** 1M users in ~60 s ≈ 17k req/s of page loads plus a burst of hold attempts; only 50k successful holds.

**Key ideas:**
- **Waiting room / virtual queue** in front of on-sale: admit users at a rate the booking system can handle (say 2,000
  per second). This is backpressure at the front door (lesson 7.6).
- **Seat holds** with expiry: `UPDATE seats SET held_by=$u, hold_until=now()+'10 min' WHERE id=$s AND (held_by IS NULL OR hold_until < now())`.
  The conditional update makes double holds impossible.
- Shard seat inventory **by event** (each event's seats on one shard), keeping each hold a single-row operation.
- **Payment with an idempotency key** = hold id; record the booking and mark the seat sold in one transaction after
  payment succeeds; release on payment failure or expiry.
- Confirmation emails and ticket PDFs via a **queue**.
- CDN for the event pages; the cache never decides seat availability ("cache for display, verify at the moment of
  truth").
- Failure plan: what if payments are slow (holds extend, then expire), what if the primary fails mid-sale (failover,
  idempotent retries).
</details>
